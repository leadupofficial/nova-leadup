# Store listing copy

Submission metadata for Google Play and App Store Connect. Nothing here is generated from
code, so it cannot go stale the way a comment can — but every claim below is one the app
actually implements, which is the point: earlier rounds removed a health-data claim, an
"Enterprise SSO" notice, a wake-word phrase that was never installed, push/SMS/email
notification switches with no sender behind them, and a privacy control that controlled
nothing. Anything added here must be checkable in the app.

## Full description (Play: 4000 characters max · App Store: 4000)

```
NOVA is a voice companion. You talk to it the way you would talk to a person, and it
answers out loud — and it remembers what you asked it to.

TALK, DON'T TYPE
Press the mic and speak. NOVA transcribes as you go, replies in a natural voice, and you
can interrupt it mid-sentence. On Android you can also switch on a wake word so it is
ready without you reaching for the phone; the wake word is detected on your device, and a
persistent notification tells you whenever it is listening.

IT KEEPS TRACK
Ask for a reminder, a task or a note and it is saved — "remind me tomorrow at 10",
"add a task: send Kumar the proposal". Reminders arrive as notifications on your phone,
and NOVA can read the daily briefing aloud each morning: your tasks, your reminders and
what is coming up.

IT REMEMBERS, WITH YOUR PERMISSION
NOVA can hold on to the things you tell it — a preference, a decision, a contact — and
use them later. Every memory is something you approved, and you can see and delete them
at any time.

MEETINGS AND CALL RECORDINGS
Record a meeting in the app, or point NOVA at a call recording your own dialer already
saved, and it produces a transcript and a short summary: what was decided, what needs
doing next, who was mentioned.

BUILT FOR INDIA
Speaks and understands 23 languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam,
Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, Odia, Assamese and Nepali — and mixed speech
like Hinglish and Tanglish, which is how people actually talk.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your privacy controls decide what is kept. Turn off "Save recordings" or "Save
  transcripts" and NOVA uses them to answer, then does not keep them.
• Auto-delete removes recordings and transcripts on the schedule you choose — or never.
• Delete your account from inside the app whenever you want, and everything goes with it.
• No advertising identifier, no ads, no data brokers, no location, no contacts, no
  calendar, no camera and no health data.
• Read the full policy at https://nova.leadup.in/privacy

WHAT NOVA DOES NOT DO
It does not place calls, send SMS, read your screen, toggle Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, or control
other apps. It does not read notifications unless you grant Notification Access and
switch the assistant on, app by app — and it stores none of what it reads.

REQUIREMENTS
An internet connection is needed: your speech is transcribed on NOVA's servers, and there
is no on-device model in this version. That is why "Cloud processing" cannot be switched
off, and the app says so rather than offering a switch that would not change anything.

Questions or problems: support@leadup.tech
```

## Notes for whoever fills in the console

* **Do not** describe the app as working offline, as processing speech on-device, or as
  sending push notifications — none of those is implemented.
* The **Support URL** to enter is `https://nova.leadup.in/support`; the console page
  exists and is public.
* The **privacy policy URL** is `https://nova.leadup.in/privacy`.
* The **account-deletion URL** for the Play Data safety form is
  `https://nova.leadup.in/delete-account`.
* Both of those URLs answer only once the current revision is deployed. Until then they
  redirect to a login page, which the stores treat as broken.
